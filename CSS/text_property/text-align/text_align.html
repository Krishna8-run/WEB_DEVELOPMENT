Tony Stark

Iron Man, whose real name is Anthony "Tony" Stark, is a genius billionaire, industrialist, and a founding member of the Avengers superhero team in the Marvel Comics universe and the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). He uses his intellect to create a high-tech armored suit that grants him superhuman abilities.

Character Profile

Enemies: The Mandarin (archenemy in comics), Obadiah Stane (Iron Monger), Justin Hammer, and various other villains and criminal organizations like Hydra and the Ten Rings.
